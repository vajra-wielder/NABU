Check the drive

Pulled out while busy, or on a loose connection, a drive can come back with damaged files. Every bundle of articles carries checksums, so NABU can read them all and tell exactly which articles are damaged. Those can be copied back from another NABU drive with the same Wikipedia (one made with Make a copy): plug both in and press Repair. Indexes are rebuilt from the dump; pictures are not checked (they can be downloaded again).
