No Wi-Fi network?

  • Any phone can make one: turn on its hotspot (it works without mobile data or signal), join it from this computer and the other devices, then open the address above.
  • Windows can make one itself: Settings → Network & internet → Mobile hotspot (on some computers it needs an internet connection to start).
  • Linux: in the Wi-Fi settings, "Turn on Wi-Fi hotspot".
  • The first time, Windows or the Mac may ask whether NABU may use the network: allow it on private networks.
