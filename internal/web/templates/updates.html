Get & update Wikipedia

Wikimedia publishes a new snapshot ("dump") of every Wikipedia once a month, on the 1st. NABU downloads the compressed pages-articles-multistream dump and its index straight from {{.Mirror}}, checks every byte against Wikimedia's SHA-1 checksums, and reads it in place. No conversion and no extra copy.
