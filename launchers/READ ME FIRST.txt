NABU - Wikipedia, anywhere, offline
===================================

Named after Nabu, the Babylonian god of scribes and keeper of tablets.
This drive holds a copy of Wikipedia and a small program that shows it
in your web browser. No internet and no installation needed.


START
-----

  Windows ......... double-click  "Start NABU (Windows).bat"
  Mac ............. double-click  "Start NABU (Mac).command"
                    (first time: right-click it, choose Open, then Open)
  Linux ........... run  sh start-nabu-linux.sh
                    (or double-click it and choose "Run in Terminal")

Your browser opens on the NABU library. A small window stays open while
NABU runs: keep it open while you read, and close it when you are done.
You can also click "Turn off NABU" at the bottom of any NABU page. Then
it is safe to remove the drive.

If the browser does not open by itself, go to:  http://localhost:8080


GETTING OR UPDATING WIKIPEDIA
-----------------------------

Wikipedia lives in the "library" folder as .zim files. When the
computer is online, NABU checks for a newer edition each time it starts
and offers to download it ("Get & update" page). Downloads resume where
they left off if the connection drops, and are verified when complete.

Sizes (English, approximate):
  complete, with pictures (maxi) ....... about 110 GB
  complete, no pictures (nopic) ........ about  50 GB
  introductions only (mini) ............ about  12 GB
Other languages are smaller. Big files are saved in 4 GB pieces
(.zimaa, .zimab, ...) so they work on any drive format.

You can also copy any Kiwix .zim file into the library folder yourself.
They are listed at  https://download.kiwix.org/zim/


SHARE WITH A ROOM
-----------------

Start NABU from a terminal with  --lan  and other phones and computers
on the same Wi-Fi can read it too, at the address NABU shows.


WHICH COMPUTERS
---------------

Windows 7 or newer, macOS 10.13 or newer, and Linux, on Intel/AMD
(32- and 64-bit) and ARM processors (including Raspberry Pi and Apple
silicon). Any modern web browser.
