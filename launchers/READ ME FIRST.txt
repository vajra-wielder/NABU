NABU - Wikipedia, anywhere, offline
===================================

Named after Nabu, the Babylonian god of scribes and keeper of tablets.
This drive holds Wikipedia, exactly as Wikimedia publishes it, and a
small program that shows it in your web browser. No internet and no
installation needed.


START
-----

  Windows ......... double-click  "Start NABU (Windows).bat"
  Mac ............. double-click  "Start NABU (Mac).command"
                    (if macOS refuses the first time: macOS 15 or newer:
                    System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway;
                    older macOS: right-click it, choose Open, then Open)
  Linux ........... run  sh start-nabu-linux.sh
                    (or double-click it and choose "Run in Terminal")

Your browser opens on the NABU library. A small window stays open while
NABU runs: keep it open while you read, and close it when you are done.
You can also click "Turn off NABU" at the bottom of any NABU page. Then
it is safe to remove the drive.

If the browser does not open by itself, go to:  http://localhost:8080

If Windows says "Smart App Control blocked" NABU, or the computer is in
"S mode", or a school/office computer forbids programs from USB sticks,
NABU cannot run there. Run it on another computer with --lan instead
(see SHARE WITH A ROOM): the locked one can still read it in its browser.

CHROMEBOOK: turn on "Linux development environment" in Settings, share
the drive with Linux (right-click it in Files), then in the Linux
terminal run:
    sh /mnt/chromeos/removable/<drive name>/start-nabu-linux.sh --lan
and open  http://penguin.linux.test:8080  in Chrome. (School-managed
Chromebooks often have Linux turned off; then read from another
computer running NABU with --lan.)


GETTING OR UPDATING WIKIPEDIA
-----------------------------

Each Wikipedia is two files from Wikimedia in the "library" folder:

  enwiki-20261101-pages-articles-multistream.xml.bz2        (the articles)
  enwiki-20261101-pages-articles-multistream-index.txt.bz2  (where each one is)

When the computer is online, NABU asks Wikimedia for a newer dump each
time it starts and offers it on the "Get & update" page. Downloads resume
where they stopped and are checked against Wikimedia's checksums.

The first time NABU sees a new dump it spends a few minutes building a
search index next to it (about 2% of the dump's size). Only once.

Sizes of the ten Wikipedias NABU offers (November 2026):
  English 25.4 GB   French 6.9 GB    Russian 5.9 GB    Spanish 5.1 GB
  Chinese 3.4 GB    Portuguese 2.7 GB   Arabic 2.1 GB  Persian 1.5 GB
  Indonesian 1.3 GB   Danish 0.5 GB              All ten: about 55 GB
Together these languages are official across ~91% of the world's land.

Dumps contain the text of every article. Pictures are shown from
Wikimedia when the computer is online, and as labelled frames when not.


SHARE WITH A ROOM
-----------------

Start NABU from a terminal with  --lan  and other phones and computers
on the same Wi-Fi can read it too, at the address NABU shows.


WHICH COMPUTERS
---------------

Windows 7 or newer, macOS 10.13 or newer, and Linux, on Intel/AMD
(32- and 64-bit, back to the Pentium III) and ARM processors (including
Raspberry Pi and Apple silicon), with 1 GB of memory or more. Any web
browser from the last ten years, and Internet Explorer 11.
Details: docs/COMPATIBILITY.md in the source code.
